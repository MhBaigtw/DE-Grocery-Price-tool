# Retired — 2026-09-12

This dashboard is **kept as a record and is never published again.** The Netlify build
refuses to publish it: `scripts/netlify_build.sh` stops if `_site/dashboard` exists.

## Why it is retired rather than rebuilt

1. **The analysis now has one surviving pairwise comparison.** Walmart against Metro, on
   identical national-brand products, both priced in North York, Toronto. Every other
   pairwise comparison the dashboard charted involved Save-On-Foods, whose prices come from a
   store in **Kamloops, BC**. Those were comparisons between two cities, and with one store
   per chain, regional pricing cannot be separated from store pricing. They are withdrawn as
   **W6** (`docs/phase-2-findings.md`).
2. **The writeup already carries the surviving comparison**, with its qualifiers in the
   sentence that states it. A dashboard would repeat one number.
3. **Every chart is a surface where a withdrawn claim can resurface.** A chart outlives the
   context that justified it: it gets screenshotted, embedded and linked without the
   withdrawal beside it. One fewer surface is one fewer place for W6 to come back.

## What this directory still is

The record of what Phase 3 published, generated by `analysis/phase3/D1_dashboard_extract.sql`.
Its figures are stamped to the build they were computed under. **The pairwise chart includes
withdrawn comparisons** — read it as history, not as a finding.
